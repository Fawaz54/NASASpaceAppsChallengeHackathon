# Demo script (about four minutes)

Use two windows, or two laptops on the same network: the crew screen
(http://localhost:8000) as "the ship" and the doctor screen
(http://localhost:8000/doctor.html) as "Earth". Set the profile's location
to Mars base, so the delay is 19 minutes each way, played as 19 seconds.

Press "Reset demo" at the bottom of the home screen before you start.

1. **Profile.** Show the profile: pre-flight baseline, medical notes and
   location. "The app knows what is normal for this person before launch."
2. **Normal day.** Home screen: green body figure. Tap the chest to show the
   reading, what it means and the 30-day chart.
3. **Daily check-in.** Mood, sleep, one measurement, save. On the doctor
   screen the report arrives after the delay and is acknowledged
   automatically because everything is green.
4. **Slow drift.** Press "Heart drift". Nothing is over a limit, but the
   chest turns amber because the last week has moved away from this
   person's baseline. Do a check-in: this report needs review, so the
   "doctor" types feedback and it arrives on the crew screen after the delay.
5. **Automatic alert.** Press "Solar particle event". The dose rate climbs,
   the outline round the body turns red, a tone plays and the checklist
   appears with "Read steps aloud". Point out the real NASA DONKI event it
   names. Tick the steps.
6. **Emergency.** Press "I need help". Crew and Earth are alerted at once.
   Type a description, let it fill in the questions, answer the danger
   signs, and show the steps. The doctor's reply arrives later; the crew
   member already knew what to do.
7. **Offline.** Turn off the wifi and repeat any step. Everything still works.
8. **Ask.** Open "Ask" and ask why a checklist was shown. Explain that the
   AI only explains what the written rules decided.

Other scenario buttons: "Fever", "Sleep and mood decline", "Missed exercise".
